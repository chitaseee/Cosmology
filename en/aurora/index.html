Auroras: Solar Particles Interacting with Earth’s Atmosphere

Auroras occur when charged particles guided by Earth’s magnetic field collide with atmospheric gases, producing colourful light.

Content reviewed: 8 November 2026 · Educational article
In brief: Auroras occur when charged particles guided by Earth’s magnetic field collide with atmospheric gases, producing colourful light.

Features and structure

Oxygen can produce green or red emissions, while nitrogen contributes blue and purple hues. Geomagnetic storms can extend auroras to lower latitudes.
