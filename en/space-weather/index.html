Space Weather: How Solar Activity Affects Earth

Space weather describes changes in near-Earth space driven by solar wind, flares and coronal mass ejections. These events can affect satellites and technology.

Educational guide · Reviewed: 8 November 2026

In brief: Space weather describes changes in near-Earth space driven by solar wind, flares and coronal mass ejections. These events can affect satellites and technology.

Key properties and structure

Solar particles and magnetic fields travel through space and interact with Earth’s magnetosphere and ionosphere. These processes can produce auroras and geomagnetic storms.

Scientific processes and evidence

Severe events can affect navigation, radio communication, satellites and power grids. Solar monitoring provides early warnings, although precise prediction remains difficult.

Why it matters for astronomy

Research on Space Weather: How Solar Activity Affects Earth combines telescope observations, spacecraft measurements and physical models. Every conclusion must be interpreted with the uncertainties and limits of the available data.

Frequently asked question

What does this topic teach us about the universe?

It helps scientists understand the physical and historical processes that shape celestial objects. Scientific interpretation must rely on observations and account for the limits of each method.
