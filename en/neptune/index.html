Neptune: A Distant Planet with Fast Winds

Neptune is the most distant recognised planet in the Solar System. One Neptunian year lasts nearly 165 Earth years.

Content reviewed: 8 November 2026 · Educational article
In brief: Neptune is the most distant recognised planet in the Solar System. One Neptunian year lasts nearly 165 Earth years.

Features and structure

Despite the cold, Neptune’s atmosphere is dynamic and has very fast winds. Triton, its largest moon, follows a retrograde orbit.
